1 / 25 · D041

Classify September customer deposits.

Since DPI hadn't yet delivered its services, but had received the customers money, DPI still owed the service/goods, so €90,000 was a liability. Calling it revenue, would make the profit too high, which would be incorrect.

2 / 25 · D042

Classify New bank borrowing.

It was a loan from the bank, not a money that DPI had earned doing their services to customer. As well loans have to be repaid, and that makes this a liability, it in no way increases profit.

3 / 25 · D043

Classify Packaging machine.

The machine will be used over long-term period, and it will help DPI produce more and deliver future revenue. So it is a long-term asset which needs to be capitalised at first, and over its useful life, depreciated. At the purchase date, equipment increases by €60,000 and cash decreases by €60,000, so total assets do not change.

4 / 25 · D044

Classify Photo booth.

Same as the packaging machine, the photo booth will be useful for more than one period and more than one event, so it is also capitalised as long- term asset and depreciated over its useful life.

5 / 25 · D045

Classify Machine belt and calibration.

Because it was maintenence expense to make the machine work at its usual condition, not to enhance it or extend its useful life .

7 / 25 · D047
